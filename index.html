Citizen's Charter 2026

The official guide to frontline services across the Municipal Government of Mabini — what each office offers, what to bring, what it costs, and how long it takes.

A Citizen's Charter is an official document, required under the Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act (RA 11032), that lists a government office's services along with requirements, fees, processing time, and the person responsible at each step. This charter covers all frontline offices of the Municipality of Mabini.
